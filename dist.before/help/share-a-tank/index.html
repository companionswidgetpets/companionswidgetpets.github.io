Can I share my tank with a friend?

Yes. The ⋯ menu's Share this tank makes a code and a picture of the tank. A friend who opens the code in Companions gets your water, props and complication on their shelf, with their own fish swimming in it.

Updated November 9, 2026 · Companions 4.5

Tap ⋯ at the top of the main page and choose Share this tank. Companions makes two things and opens the share sheet:

  • A picture of the tank, laid out as a card with the app’s name, ready for Messages or a story.
  • A code that holds the tank’s design: its style, every prop and where it stands, and its complication if it has one.

Send either. Someone with Companions who opens the code gets your tank on their shelf for that size, exactly as you arranged it, and their own fish swim in it. The code holds nothing about you or your fish; names and fish never leave your device. See the privacy policy.

If the tank has a complication (the time, the date, the weather, steps or the Moon), the person receiving it needs All Fish to keep it; otherwise they get the tank without the corner, with a note saying so.

You can also save the code’s QR as a picture to Photos; iOS asks permission to add it the first time.
