Do I need a subscription?

No. Everything in Companions can be bought once: each pack is a one-time purchase, and All Fish has a lifetime option. The monthly plan is there for people who prefer it.

Updated November 9, 2026 · Companions 4.5

No. Companions was made for people who are tired of apps that charge every week to keep a pet alive.

  • The app, eight fish, every style, every prop and every widget size are free.
  • Each pack of five fish is bought once and kept.
  • All Fish Lifetime is bought once and opens every pack, now and in the future, plus the complications.

The All Fish Monthly plan exists for people who would rather pay a little while they use it than more up front. It unlocks the same things as the lifetime purchase for as long as it runs. If you stop it, nothing is taken away: the fish you added stay, and the complications you set keep working. You just can’t add more fish from the paid packs, or choose new complications, until you renew or buy the lifetime unlock.

You manage the plan where you manage every subscription: Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone.
