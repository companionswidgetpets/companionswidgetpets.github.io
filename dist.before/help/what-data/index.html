What does Companions collect about me?

Nothing personal. There's no account, no advertising and no tracking; your tank and your fish's names stay on your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off in Settings.

Updated November 9, 2026 · Companions 4.5

As little as an app can. Companions has no account to create and no server of its own. The only things it saves are the fish in your tanks, the names you give them and your tank designs, and it saves them on your device, in a storage area the widget shares, where they stay.

What it does send:

  • Anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: events such as the app opening, a fish being added or a pack being unlocked, with the device model, iOS version, language, app version and the country estimated from your connection. Each installation has a random identifier, not linked to you, your Apple Account or your fish. Turn it off with Share Usage Data in Settings.
  • If you choose the Weather complication, your approximate location goes to Apple’s weather service for the forecast, and nowhere else.
  • If you choose Step Goal, the app reads today’s step count from Health and keeps it on your device for the widget.

Purchases are handled by the App Store; Companions never sees your payment details. The full privacy policy is the same one shown in the app.
