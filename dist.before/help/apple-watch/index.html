Does Companions work on Apple Watch?

Yes. The Companions watch app shows your small tank on your wrist, with the same fish, props and style, synced from your iPhone. The watch keeps its own clock, so a tank's complication is left off there.

Updated November 9, 2026 · Companions 4.5

Yes. Companions comes with an Apple Watch app. It shows your small tank: the same water, the same props and the same fish swimming the same way, synced from your iPhone over the connection the two already share.

To get it, open the Watch app on your iPhone and look for Companions under Available Apps, or let it install automatically if your watch installs new apps on its own. In Companions on the iPhone, Settings › Apple Watch shows exactly what the watch will show, on a drawing of the watch, with Save and use tank to pick which small tank it is.

A few things are different on the wrist:

  • The watch shows the small tank only, the one in use on the small shelf.
  • Complications (the time, date, weather, steps and Moon in a corner) are left off: the watch has a clock of its own.
  • The watch doesn’t feed the fish or let you add them; that happens on the iPhone and syncs over.

If the Settings page says No watch connected, make sure your watch is paired to this iPhone and nearby, and that Companions is installed on it.
