Getting started

How do I add the Companions widget to my Home Screen?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for Companions, choose the small, medium or large tank and tap Add Widget.

Full answer
Which widget sizes does Companions come in?

Three: small (a square), medium (twice as wide) and large (twice as wide and twice as tall). Each size keeps its own fish, and the app previews all three before you place one.

Full answer
How many fish can I keep in a tank?

Five per tank. Each widget size (small, medium, large) is its own tank with its own five, so you can keep fifteen fish across the three sizes.

Full answer
Which fish are free?

Eight: every tank can have the Tang, Sardine and Puffer, and the Freshwater Pack (Neon, Guppy, Goldfish, Angelfish and Betta) comes with the app. The other packs are one-time purchases.

Full answer

The widget

Do the fish actually move in the widget?

Yes. The fish swim continuously in the Home Screen widget, smoothly, without the app running, because their paths are built from the same kind of motion iOS uses to sweep a clock widget's hands.

Full answer
Does Companions drain my battery?

No. The widget runs no code of its own: iOS draws the fish from a description the app hands it, the same way it sweeps a clock widget's hands. Nothing runs in the background.

Full answer
Can each widget size have different fish?

Yes, since Companions 4.5. The small, medium and large tanks each keep their own five fish, so a small reef tank and a large pond tank can share a Home Screen.

Full answer
Can I put the aquarium on my Lock Screen?

Not yet. Companions makes Home Screen widgets in the three colour sizes; the Lock Screen takes small monochrome widgets, which wouldn't do the fish justice. StandBy shows the tank while your iPhone charges.

Full answer

Fish and packs

How much does Companions cost?

The app is free with eight fish. Other fish come in packs bought once and kept for good, or all together with All Fish, as a one-time lifetime purchase or a monthly plan. Prices are shown in the app in your currency.

Full answer
Do I need a subscription?

No. Everything in Companions can be bought once: each pack is a one-time purchase, and All Fish has a lifetime option. The monthly plan is there for people who prefer it.

Full answer
How do I rename or remove a fish?

Tap the fish in the tray under the tank. From there you can change its name or remove it. Removing a fish deletes it from your device; nothing else happens to the tank.

Full answer
Do I have to feed the fish?

No. Nothing in Companions needs feeding, cleaning or attention, and no fish ever dies. Feeding is a little game you can play when you feel like it: tap the fork and knife, pinch some food into the water and watch them chase it.

Full answer
Can I make the fish bigger, smaller, faster or slower?

Yes. Settings has a Fish Size control with five steps from 75% to 125% and a Swim Speed control from a quarter slower to a quarter faster. Both apply to the app and the widgets alike.

Full answer

Tanks, styles and props

How do I change the tank's colour?

Open the ⋯ menu, choose Edit this tank, and pick a style from the menu at the top: Minimal, Twilight, Pond, Reed, Blossom, Dark, Pumpkin or Lavender. The widget follows.

Full answer
Can I decorate the tank?

Yes. The Edit Tank page has 19 props: ferns, corals, shells, rocks, a pineapple house, a Moai and more. Drag them where you like, resize, flip, undo and redo. Each tank keeps its own arrangement.

Full answer
Can I have more than one tank?

Yes. My Tanks keeps a shelf of tanks for each widget size, one in use at a time. Duplicate a tank, edit the copy, and switch which one your widget shows whenever you like.

Full answer
Can I share my tank with a friend?

Yes. The ⋯ menu's Share this tank makes a code and a picture of the tank. A friend who opens the code in Companions gets your water, props and complication on their shelf, with their own fish swimming in it.

Full answer

Complications

What are complications?

Something useful in a corner of the tank, in white over the water: the time, the date, the weather, today's steps against your goal, or tonight's Moon. One per tank, chosen on the Edit Tank page, included with All Fish.

Full answer

StandBy, Apple Watch, Mac and iPad

Does Companions work in StandBy?

Yes. StandBy shows small widgets while your iPhone charges on its side. Turn StandBy on, lay the phone sideways on a charger, touch and hold the widget stack, tap + and add Companions.

Full answer
Does Companions work on Apple Watch?

Yes. The Companions watch app shows your small tank on your wrist, with the same fish, props and style, synced from your iPhone. The watch keeps its own clock, so a tank's complication is left off there.

Full answer
Can I put the widget on my Mac?

Yes, through iPhone widgets on Mac. Turn on Use iPhone Widgets in System Settings › Desktop & Dock, then Edit Widgets on the desktop and drag Companions out of the iPhone section.

Full answer
Does Companions work on iPad?

Yes. Companions is a native iPad app with the same Home Screen widgets in small, medium and large, and your packs come with you under the same Apple Account.

Full answer

Purchases and privacy

What does Companions collect about me?

Nothing personal. There's no account, no advertising and no tracking; your tank and your fish's names stay on your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off in Settings.

Full answer
I bought a pack but it shows as locked. What now?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. If a purchase is still pending with Apple, it unlocks when Apple confirms it.

Full answer
Which languages does Companions speak?

Twelve: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. The app follows your iPhone's language, or pick one in Settings.

Full answer

When something looks wrong

The widget isn't showing my latest fish or tank. What should I do?

Open the app once, which asks iOS to redraw its widgets. If that doesn't help, remove the widget and add it again, or restart your iPhone; iOS sometimes holds on to an old picture.

Full answer
Why is the weather complication empty?

The weather needs your location once: open the Complications sheet and tap Location under Settings, then allow it. Give it a moment to fetch the forecast. Indoors with no fix, or with Location Services off, it stays blank.

Full answer
Why does the widget look lighter than the tank in the app?

iOS lays a translucent white highlight and a bright rim over every Home Screen widget. Companions draws its widget slightly darker in advance so that, once iOS adds its glass, it matches the app.

Full answer
Why does it say the tank is full?

A tank holds five fish. The + in the tray dims when all five slots are taken; remove a fish to make room, or swipe to another widget size, which has a tank of its own.

Full answer
