Support

We'll sort it out.

Companions is made by two people, and one of us answers every email personally. Write tocompanionswidgetpets@gmail.com, or use the Email button in the app's Settings, which fills in the details for you.

Before you write

Most questions are answered on the help pages. The ones we hear most:

  • How do I add the Companions widget to my Home Screen?
  • Do the fish actually move in the widget?
  • How much does Companions cost?
  • Does Companions work in StandBy?
  • Does Companions drain my battery?
  • How many fish can I keep in a tank?
  • Do I have to feed the fish?
  • Which fish are free?

What to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings › General › About).
  • The Companions version, shown at the bottom of the app's Settings.
  • What you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot or a screen recording helps enormously.

Purchases and refunds

Packs and All Fish are sold by Apple through the App Store, so refunds go through Apple: visitreportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. If a pack you bought shows as locked, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases; make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

Privacy

Companions keeps your tank on your device and collects no personal information. The privacy policy has the details.

Something you'd love to see?

A fish, a prop, a style, a place the widget should live: tell us. Many of the packs began as an email.
