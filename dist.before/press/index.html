Press kit

Writing about Companions?

Everything here may be quoted and the pictures used freely in coverage of the app. For anything else, email companionswidgetpets@gmail.com.

In one sentence

Companions is a free iPhone app that puts a little animated aquarium in a Home Screen widget: pick your fish, name them, and watch them swim every time you look at your phone.

In a paragraph

Companions is a free iPhone app that keeps a little fish tank in a Home Screen widget. You pick your fish from58 hand-drawn species, give each a name, and they swim in their own way at their own pace, every time you unlock your phone: in the widget, in StandBy while the phone charges, on your Mac desktop and on Apple Watch. There is nothing to feed and nothing can die; feeding is a game you can play if you like. Tanks come in eight water styles with 20 props to arrange, and can carry the time, the date, the weather, your step goal or the Moon in a corner. Eight fish come with the app; the rest arrive in packs bought once, or all together with All Fish. Companions has no account, no advertising and no tracking, and is translated into 12 languages. It is made by Tiaan van der Riel and Shea in South Korea.
