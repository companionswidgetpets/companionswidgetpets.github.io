Fish pack

Reef Pack

A collection of some of the most beautiful fish from earth's mighty reefs.

The Reef Pack in a Minimal tank, medium widget size.

Clownfish

The clownfish swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall.

Idol

The idol swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall, and is drawn a touch smaller than a standard fish.

Regal tang

The regal tang swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall.

Parrotfish

The parrotfish swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall.

Lionfish

The lionfish swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall.

How to add these fish

  1. Open Companions and tap the + at the end of the tray under the tank.
  2. Find the Reef Pack in the list and tap a fish.
  3. If the pack is locked, tap Unlock and buy the pack once, or All Fish for every pack. Then the fish drops into the water with a splash. Give it a name, or keep the one it came with.

A tank holds five fish, and each widget size keeps its own set, so the small, medium and large tanks can be stocked differently.
