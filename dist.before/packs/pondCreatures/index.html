Pond Creatures Pack

A collection of the little creatures that hop, wriggle and lurk among the lily pads.

The Pond Creatures Pack in a Pond tank, medium widget size.

Minnow

The minnow swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall.

Frog

Toad

Tadpole

The tadpole swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall.

How to add these fish

  1. Open Companions and tap the + at the end of the tray under the tank.
  2. Find the Pond Creatures Pack in the list and tap a fish.
  3. If the pack is locked, tap Unlock and buy the pack once, or All Fish for every pack. Then the fish drops into the water with a splash. Give it a name, or keep the one it came with.

A tank holds five fish, and each widget size keeps its own set, so the small, medium and large tanks can be stocked differently.
