How packs work

A pack is a one-time purchase through the App Store, in your local currency. Buy it on your iPhone and it is yours on your iPad too, under the same Apple Account; Restore Purchases in Settings brings it back on a new device. Nothing expires, and nothing in a tank is ever taken away.

All Fish comes two ways: a one-time lifetime unlock, or a monthly plan while you want it. Either way it opens every pack, every pack added later, and the complications (the time, date, weather, steps and Moon in a tank's corner). If a monthly plan lapses, the fish you already added keep swimming; only adding new ones from those packs waits until it is renewed.

Prices are shown in the app, under Settings, in your own currency.
