Fish pack

Shark Pack

A collection of the sleek hunters and gentle giants that rule the open ocean.

The Shark Pack in a Twilight tank, medium widget size.

Great white

The great white swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall, and is drawn 25% bigger than a standard fish.

Manta

The manta swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall, and is drawn 10% bigger than a standard fish.

Hammer

The hammer swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall, and is drawn 15% bigger than a standard fish.

Whale shark

The whale shark swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall, and is drawn 25% bigger than a standard fish.

Thresher

The thresher swims mid-water, gliding briskly with a little rise and fall, and is drawn 15% bigger than a standard fish.

How to add these fish

  1. Open Companions and tap the + at the end of the tray under the tank.
  2. Find the Shark Pack in the list and tap a fish.
  3. If the pack is locked, tap Unlock and buy the pack once, or All Fish for every pack. Then the fish drops into the water with a splash. Give it a name, or keep the one it came with.

A tank holds five fish, and each widget size keeps its own set, so the small, medium and large tanks can be stocked differently.
