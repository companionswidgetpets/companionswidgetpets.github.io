Getting started

Your fish tank in StandBy

Turn your charging iPhone into a bedside aquarium: how StandBy works, how to add the Companions widget to it, and why the Dark tank style was made for the night.

By Tiaan van der Riel · November 9, 2026 · Companions 4.5

On this page

  1. Turn StandBy on
  2. Add the tank
  3. Choosing the other widget
  4. Night and the Dark style
  5. Always-on iPhones

StandBy is the full-screen view an iPhone shows when it is charging, locked and lying on its side. It has three pages: a clock, your photos, and a pair of widgets. The Companions tank goes in that pair, and it is a lovely thing to wake up to.

Turn StandBy on

Open Settings › StandBy and make sure StandBy is on. Below it, Night Mode tints the screen red in a dark room, and on iPhones with an always-on display Always On keeps StandBy visible without a tap.

Add the tank

  1. Put your iPhone on a charger, on its side. StandBy appears after a moment; if you see the clock or photos, swipe sideways to the widget page.
  2. Touch and hold either of the two widgets until it lifts.
  3. Tap + in the top-left corner.
  4. Choose Companions, then Add Widget.
  5. Tap Done.

StandBy takes small widgets, so what you see is your small tank: the one in use on the small shelf, with its fish, props and style. Change any of them in the app and StandBy follows.

Choosing the other widget

The second widget is a stack of its own. A clock is the natural partner; the Calendar, Reminders or Battery widgets are good too. Each side can hold several widgets stacked; swipe up and down on a side to move between them.

Night and the Dark style

In a dark room, Night Mode turns StandBy red so it doesn’t light the room, and that includes the tank. If you’d rather the water looked like water, turn Night Mode off; the Dark style is the one to use then. It is as black as the Home Screen allows, with the glass highlights turned down, so a Dark tank sits quietly on a bedside table instead of glowing.

Open the tank in the app, tap ⋯ › Edit this tank, and pick Dark from the style menu. The fish that show best in it are the bright ones: the Neon, the Jellyfish, the Ghost and the Robot from the Special Pack.

Always-on iPhones

On an iPhone with an always-on display, StandBy stays up all night. iOS dims it, and when the room is dark and nothing has moved for a while it dims further, as it does for every widget. The fish keep swimming the whole time; the widget needs no app running and no network to do so.
