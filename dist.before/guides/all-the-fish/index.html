Fish

Every fish in Companions, and how each one moves

The full roster of the Companions aquarium widget: 58 species in 12 packs, which come free, which float, crawl or creep instead of swimming, and which are drawn bigger than the rest.

By Tiaan van der Riel · November 9, 2026 · Companions 4.5

On this page

  1. The roster
  2. How they move
  3. Who is drawn bigger
  4. Other things fish do

Companions has 58 creatures, drawn by hand, all facing left so they can turn to face the way they swim. They come in twelve packs. Eight are free; the rest are bought once, by the pack or all together with All Fish. Every pack has a page with its fish large and their names in twelve languages.

The roster

Stock Fish (come with the app): Tang, Sardine, Puffer.

Freshwater Pack (comes with the app): Neon, Guppy, Goldfish, Angelfish, Betta.

Reef Pack: Clownfish, Idol (the Moorish idol), Regal tang, Parrotfish, Lionfish.

Pond Pack: Koi, Gold koi, Catfish, Trout, Terrapin.

Sea Creatures Pack: Sea turtle, Seahorse, Squid, Jellyfish, Octopus.

Pond Creatures Pack: Minnow, Axolotl, Frog, Toad, Tadpole.

Shark Pack: Great white, Manta, Hammer (hammerhead), Whale shark, Thresher.

Whale Pack: Dolphin, Orca, Blue whale, Sperm whale, Humpback.

Reef Pack II: Emperor (the emperor angelfish), Butterfly (the yellow butterflyfish), Triggerfish, Dartfish, Starfish.

Freshwater Pack II: Discus, Zebrafish (the zebra danio), Rummynose, Gourami (the pearl gourami), Shrimp (a ghost shrimp).

Open Ocean Pack: Tuna, Barracuda, Mahi-mahi, Swordfish, Snapper.

Special Pack, things that aren’t fish at all and swim about like them anyway: Ghost, Skeleton, Robot, Balloon, Submarine.

How they move

Every creature has a lane, a depth in the water that is its own, and moves along it as a glide and a drift added together: a brisk to-and-fro across most of the tank, carried about by a slow one across the rest, so it wanders unevenly rather than ping-ponging. A small bob adds a little life. All of it is drawn from the creature’s identity, so a fish swims the same way in the app, in the widget and on the watch, and crosses a wide tank at the same speed as a small one rather than hurrying.

Four kinds of mover:

  • Swimmers, which is most of them: mid-water, gliding briskly, with a little rise and fall, turning to face the way they go.
  • Floaters, the Frog and the Toad: they hang anywhere a fish might, drifting slowly, and barely rise and fall. Their pictures are wide and flat, so a fish’s bob would toss them about.
  • The creeper, the Starfish: it sits on the bed and inches along it, so slowly it is hardly seen to move, leaning a little with its way rather than turning round; a starfish looks the same either way.
  • The crawler, the Shrimp: it walks the bed at a scuttle, a touch up and down as its legs go, at about a third of a fish’s pace.

The bed is the line the props stand on, so the starfish and the shrimp live among the ferns and corals.

Who is drawn bigger

The ocean giants would look toy-sized next to a sardine drawn the same size, so they get more room:

Drawn at Species
150% Blue whale, Sperm whale, Humpback
125% Great white, Whale shark, Orca
115% Hammer, Thresher
110% Manta, Dolphin, Discus, Tuna, Barracuda, Mahi-mahi, Swordfish
90% Tang, Sardine, Puffer, Idol
100% everyone else

The Fish Size setting scales every species alike on top of this, from 75% to 125%, so the whales stay half again as big as the sardines whatever you choose. A big fish is also kept a little further from the glass, so it never clips the rim.

Other things fish do

They breathe out the odd small bubble. They chase food when you feed them. Some mornings one of them says hello in a thought bubble. Tap the glass and a puff of bubbles rises. None of it is ever required of you; nothing in the tank can die.
