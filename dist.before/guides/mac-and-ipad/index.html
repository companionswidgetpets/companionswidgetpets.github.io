iPhone widgets on your Mac desktop, and Companions on iPad

Put the Companions tank among your Mac windows with iPhone widgets on Mac, and what to expect from the fish there. Plus the iPad app.

By Tiaan van der Riel · November 9, 2026 · Companions 4.5

On the Mac

Since macOS Sonoma, a Mac can show the widgets of an iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, as long as the two are near each other or on the same Wi-Fi. Companions needs no Mac app of its own for this; your iPhone’s widgets simply become available on the desktop.

  1. On the Mac, open System Settings › Desktop & Dock, scroll to Widgets, and turn on Use iPhone Widgets.
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) an empty part of the desktop and choose Edit Widgets.
  3. In the gallery, find Companions; iPhone widgets are marked with a small phone. Choose a size and drag it onto the desktop, or into Notification Centre.
  4. Click Done.

The Mac shows the widget as your iPhone draws it, so a fish you add on the phone appears on the Mac. Clicking the widget on the Mac opens Continuity to the iPhone rather than the app.

What to expect from the fish there

On the iPhone’s Home Screen the fish swim continuously, because iOS animates the widget itself. On the Mac, iPhone widgets are pictures mirrored from the phone, and how often macOS asks for a fresh one is up to macOS. So the tank on your desktop may update in steps rather than swim smoothly; that is how all iPhone widgets behave on the Mac, not something the app can change.

Desktop widgets in the way?

System Settings › Desktop & Dock › Widgets has options for showing widgets on the desktop, in Stage Manager, and for fading them when an app is in front.

On the iPad

Companions is a native iPad app with the same three widget sizes, for iPadOS 26 or later. Tanks are laid out the same way as on the iPhone, so a tank designed on one looks the same on the other. Add the widget to the iPad’s Home Screen the same way: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget.

Packs and All Fish follow your Apple Account, so anything bought on the iPhone is yours on the iPad: tap Restore Purchases in the app’s Settings if a pack shows as locked. Tanks and fish themselves are kept on each device; to carry a tank design across, use Share this tank on one and open the code on the other.
