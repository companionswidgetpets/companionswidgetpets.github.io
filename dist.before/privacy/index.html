Privacy policy

Your fish are your business.

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This is the same policy shown in the app under Settings.

The short version

Companions does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There is no account to create, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and everything the app knows about your tank stays on your device. The only thing Companions sends anywhere is anonymous usage statistics, so I can see which parts of the app are used; you can turn that off in Settings at any time.

What Companions stores

The only thing Companions saves is the tank itself:

  • The fish in your tank, and which species each one is
  • The name you give each fish
  • A random identifier for each fish, so the app can tell two fish of the same species apart

Names are stored exactly as you type them. If you name a fish after a friend or family member, that name is kept on your device like everything else, and nowhere else.

Where it is stored

Your tank is saved on your device, in a storage area that is shared between the Companions app and its Home Screen widget so the widget can show your fish. Companions never uploads this data anywhere.

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Apple's backup may include Companions' data along with the rest of your apps. Those backups are controlled by you in iOS Settings and are covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Widgets

The Companions widget shows the fish in your tank on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Like any widget, it is visible to anyone who can see your screen. If you use VoiceOver, the widget also reads out your fish's names. The widget reads your tank from the shared storage on your device; it does not send anything anywhere.

Usage statistics

To learn which parts of Companions people use, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service run by Google. These are events such as the app being opened, a screen being shown, a fish being added, a tank style being chosen or a pack being unlocked, together with general details: the device model, iOS version and language, the app's version, and the country and region estimated from your internet connection, whose address is not stored. Each installation of the app is given a random identifier so that repeat use counts as one person; it is not linked to you, your Apple Account or your fish and their names, and Companions does not use the advertising identifier or ask permission to track you.

You can turn this off with the Share Usage Data switch in Settings, and nothing further is sent. Google's handling of these statistics is described in its privacy policy atpolicies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions

Companions asks for three system permissions, each the first time you choose something that needs it, and works without any of them.

  • Adding photos to your library: iOS asks the first time you save a tank's QR code to Photos. The permission only lets the app add pictures, not see the photos you already have.
  • Reading your steps from Health: iOS asks the first time you choose the Step Goal complication. The app reads only today's step count, to draw it in your tank, and never writes to Health. The count and the goal you set are kept in the shared storage on your device so the widget can show them, and nowhere else.
  • Your location while the app is open: iOS asks when you tap Location under Settings on the Complications sheet, after choosing the Moon Phase or the Weather complication. The app uses it to tell which way up the Moon appears where you are, keeping only which hemisphere that is, and to fetch the weather there from Apple's Weather service, which receives your approximate location for the forecast and nothing else. Companions does not store your location or send it anywhere else.

Companions does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar or notifications, and it does not request permission to track you across other apps or websites.

Third-party services

Apart from Google Analytics for Firebase, described above, Companions contains no third-party software development kits: no crash reporting and no advertising.

The App Store handles downloading and updating the app, any packs you buy, and any ratings or reviews you choose to leave. Companions never sees your payment details. Those interactions are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Things you choose to share

A few buttons in Settings hand off to other apps, and only when you tap them:

  • Email opens your mail app with a new message addressed to me. Anything you write is sent by your mail provider, and I receive only what you send.
  • Share Companions opens the iOS share sheet with a link to the app. Companions does not see who you share it with.
  • Rate Companions asks Apple to show its rating prompt. Apple handles the rating; Companions does not receive it.
  • Share this tank, on the main page's menu, makes a code that holds only the tank's water and props, nothing about you or your fish, and opens the share sheet for you to send it.

Your control

You are in full control of your tank:

  • Rename or remove any fish at any time from the tray at the bottom of the tank
  • Removing a fish deletes it from your device
  • Turn off usage statistics with the Share Usage Data switch in Settings
  • Deleting the Companions app deletes the tank and everything else the app stored on your device (a device backup made earlier may still hold a copy until it is replaced)

Children

Companions does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age. The usage statistics described above are anonymous and never include names, anything typed into the app or anything that could identify a child, or anyone else.

This website

This website is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and embeds nothing from third parties; the fonts and pictures are served from the site itself. GitHub's own handling of web server logs is described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If Companions ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated in the app and the date at the top revised. The current version is always available from Settings.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or about how Companions handles your data, email me atcompanionswidgetpets@gmail.com or use the Email button in Settings.
