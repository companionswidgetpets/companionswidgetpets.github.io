8 styles

The same glass, different water.

A style colours the water and the bed; the glass, its rim and its highlights stay the same, so the small, medium and large tanks read as the same tank, just bigger. Change a tank's style on the Edit Tank page and every widget showing that tank follows.

Style 1 of 8

Minimal

The blue tank the app launched with: lighter azure at the top, settling into a bright sky blue through the middle, over a white mist. Bright, clean, and the one most fish look best in.

How to change a tank's style

  1. Open Companions and tap the ⋯ button at the top of the page, then Edit this tank.
  2. Open the style menu at the top of the Edit Tank page and pick a water. The tank changes under your finger.
  3. Tap Close. The tank saves itself, and the widget on your Home Screen updates to match.

Styles belong to tanks, not to the app: on the My Tanks page you can keep a Blossom small tank and a Dark large tank side by side, and switch which one your widget shows.
