Questions, answered

Before you ask.

How do I add the Companions widget to my Home Screen?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for Companions, choose the small, medium or large tank and tap Add Widget.

Read the full answer
Do the fish actually move in the widget?

Yes. The fish swim continuously in the Home Screen widget, smoothly, without the app running, because their paths are built from the same kind of motion iOS uses to sweep a clock widget's hands.

Read the full answer
How much does Companions cost?

The app is free with eight fish. Other fish come in packs bought once and kept for good, or all together with All Fish, as a one-time lifetime purchase or a monthly plan. Prices are shown in the app in your currency.

Read the full answer
Does Companions work in StandBy?

Yes. StandBy shows small widgets while your iPhone charges on its side. Turn StandBy on, lay the phone sideways on a charger, touch and hold the widget stack, tap + and add Companions.

Read the full answer
Does Companions drain my battery?

No. The widget runs no code of its own: iOS draws the fish from a description the app hands it, the same way it sweeps a clock widget's hands. Nothing runs in the background.

Read the full answer
How many fish can I keep in a tank?

Five per tank. Each widget size (small, medium, large) is its own tank with its own five, so you can keep fifteen fish across the three sizes.

Read the full answer
Do I have to feed the fish?

No. Nothing in Companions needs feeding, cleaning or attention, and no fish ever dies. Feeding is a little game you can play when you feel like it: tap the fork and knife, pinch some food into the water and watch them chase it.

Read the full answer
Which fish are free?

Eight: every tank can have the Tang, Sardine and Puffer, and the Freshwater Pack (Neon, Guppy, Goldfish, Angelfish and Betta) comes with the app. The other packs are one-time purchases.

Read the full answer
