A cute widget is the difference between a phone and *your* phone. A little fish swimming past the Calendar, a pixel cat dozing in the Dynamic Island, your best friend's face where the weather used to be: small things, seen a hundred times a day.

These are the cutest widget apps for iPhone right now. We make the first one, Companions; the rest are apps we'd happily put on our own Home Screens, described from their App Store listings as of November 2026.

## What makes a widget cute

- **Something alive.** A character, a pet, a fish: something that looks back at you.
- **A bit of movement.** Most widgets are still pictures, so anything that moves feels like magic.
- **Soft colours** that suit your wallpaper rather than fight it.
- **Little surprises**, like a greeting, a new photo or a pet that has changed since you last looked.

## 1. Companions: a tiny aquarium that really swims

Companions puts a fish tank in a Home Screen widget, and the fish swim all day, turning to face the way they go and letting out the odd bubble, with the app closed. All 58 species are drawn by hand: clownfish, seahorses, a smiling axolotl, a frog that floats instead of swimming, a pufferfish, a blue whale, and a few things that have no business in a tank, like a ghost and a little submarine.

The cute details are everywhere. Name each fish after anyone you like. Some mornings one of them says hello in a thought bubble. Tap the glass and bubbles rise. Choose pink Blossom water or pale Lavender, put a pineapple house on the bed, and switch the app icon to match.

The widget is free in small, medium and large, with eight fish and every style and prop. No ads, no account. More fish come in packs you buy once.

## 2. Pixel Pals: a pet in the Dynamic Island

Pixel-art animals that live in the Dynamic Island, on the Lock Screen, on the Home Screen and in StandBy. A dog and a cat come free, with more in Premium. Watching a tiny dog trot along the top of your screen while you text is hard to beat.

## 3. Mochi Pets: a pocket pet to look after

A round little pixel pet in an interactive widget: feed it, pet it, give it a bath and put it to bed, right from the Home Screen. It shows in StandBy too. Cute with a bit of responsibility attached, since it can get grumpy if you forget it.

## 4. CuteKit: cute themes and tiny games

CuteKit is a whole cute toolkit: animated widgets, themes, wallpapers and a few small games you play inside the widgets. It works in StandBy as well. Good if you want everything on your Home Screen to match.

## 5. Widgetsmith: make your own

Widgetsmith lets you design widgets yourself: clocks, calendars, photos, the weather, in your own fonts and colours. It also has DigiPet photo frames, little characters (a cat, a cow, a monster and a strawberry) that hold your photos. Free, with a premium plan for extras like weather.

## 6. Locket Widget: your friends' photos

Locket puts photos from your closest friends straight onto your Home Screen: they take a picture, and a moment later it appears in your widget. Not cute in the pixel-pet sense, but there is nothing sweeter than your best friend's silly face between your apps.

## 7. Finch: a bird who grows with you

In Finch you look after a little bird by looking after yourself: complete small self-care goals and it grows. Its widget keeps the bird in view.

## 8. Widgetable: a pet for two

Widgetable shares widgets between friends and couples, including a pet you raise together. Very cute if you have someone to share it with.

## 9. WidgetClub: theme packs

WidgetClub sells complete Home Screen themes: icons, wallpapers and widgets that match, including GIF and video widgets.

## Bonus: Duolingo's Duo

Not exactly cute, but very funny: Duolingo's streak widget shows its green owl getting more and more desperate as midnight approaches and your lesson isn't done.

## How to make your whole Home Screen cute

1. **Pick one colour** and stick to it: wallpaper, widgets and icons.
2. **Choose one big widget as the centrepiece.** A medium or large Companions tank works well at the top of the screen.
3. **Tint your icons.** Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Customise**, and choose tinted icons in a shade that matches your wallpaper.
4. **Match the details.** In Companions, a Blossom tank with the Blossom app icon, or a Lavender tank next to a lilac wallpaper.

## Questions people ask

### What is the cutest widget app for iPhone?

It depends what you find cute. For something alive that moves, Companions' swimming fish and Pixel Pals' pixel animals are hard to beat. For matching themes, CuteKit and WidgetClub. For people you love, Locket.

### Are cute widget apps free?

Most are free to download with something to buy. Companions' widget is free in every size with eight fish; Pixel Pals includes a dog and a cat for free. Theme apps tend to sell subscriptions, so check before you commit.

### How do I add a cute widget to my iPhone?

Open the app once, then touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, find the app, pick a size and tap **Add Widget**. Here is [the full guide](/blog/add-the-widget/).
