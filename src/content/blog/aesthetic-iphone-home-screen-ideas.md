An aesthetic Home Screen isn't about having more on it. It's about choosing one colour, one centrepiece and a little breathing room, and letting everything else follow. Here are eight complete looks to copy, each built around a Companions aquarium in matching water, with the wallpaper, icons and layout that go with it.

## Before you start: the three settings that matter

- **The icon look.** Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Customise**. Choose **Default**, **Dark**, **Clear** or **Tinted**; Tinted lets you pick any colour, with an eyedropper to borrow one from your wallpaper. **Large** icons hide the app names, for a cleaner look.
- **The glass.** iOS 26 and 27 draw icons and widgets as glass. In iOS 27, a Liquid Glass slider in **Settings › Appearance** runs from very clear to fully tinted. (On iOS 26, it's under **Settings › Display & Brightness › Liquid Glass**.)
- **The tank.** In Companions, tap **⋯**, then **Edit this tank**, and choose the water from the style menu. **Settings › App Icon** has six icons to match.

A good thing to know: when your icons are set to **Clear** or **Tinted**, iOS swaps the tank's water for its own glass, see-through or washed in your tint, and the fish and props swim over it in their own colours. So a Clear Home Screen puts the fish right over your wallpaper.

## Layout tips that work for every look

1. **One centrepiece.** A single medium or large widget at the top looks deliberate; four small ones look busy.
2. **Leave space.** Since iOS 18, apps and widgets can sit anywhere on the grid, so an empty row is allowed. Use it.
3. **Keep the Dock tidy.** Four apps you use every day, and nothing you don't.
4. **Make it one colour.** The wallpaper, the icon tint and the tank's water should all agree.
5. **Swap with the seasons.** Companions keeps a shelf of tanks for each size under **⋯ › My Tanks**, so you can keep a pink tank, an autumn tank and a dark tank and switch in a second.

## Questions people ask

### How do I make my iPhone Home Screen aesthetic?

Pick one colour and build around it: a matching wallpaper, icons set to Tinted in that colour (Edit, then Customise), and one large widget as a centrepiece, such as an aquarium in matching water. Leave some empty space.

### How do I change the colour of my iPhone icons?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Customise**, and choose **Tinted**. Pick a colour with the slider, or use the eyedropper to take one from your wallpaper.

### Can widgets match my wallpaper?

Yes. With Clear icons, many widgets become glass that shows your wallpaper through it. In Companions, the water turns to glass and the fish swim over your wallpaper; or choose one of the eight water styles to match it instead.
