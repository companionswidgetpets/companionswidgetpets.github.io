A tank looks best with a theme: fish that belong together, water that suits them, and a few props that tell a little story. Here are twelve aquariums to build in the Companions widget, from a reef everyone will recognise to a tank full of things that have no business being in water.

Each one lists its fish (a tank holds five), its water style and its props. Two cost nothing at all.

## How to build any of these

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open Companions and swipe to the size you want: small, medium or large. Each size is its own tank, with its own five fish.</li>
<li>Tap the <strong>+</strong> at the end of the tray under the tank to add each fish.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>⋯</strong>, then <strong>Edit this tank</strong>. Pick the water from the style menu at the top, then <strong>Add prop</strong> for each prop. Drag, pinch to resize, flip.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Close</strong>. The tank saves itself and your widget updates.</li>
</ol>

[Designing a tank](/blog/design-a-tank/) has every detail of the Edit Tank page.

## 1. The reef from Finding Nemo

**Fish:** Clownfish, Regal tang, Idol (the Moorish idol), Starfish, Puffer. **Water:** Minimal. **Props:** Anemone, Coral Reef, Brain Coral.

A clownfish, a blue tang, a Moorish idol, a starfish and a pufferfish: if that line-up reminds you of a certain film, that's the idea. Put the clownfish's anemone on one side, and name them however you like. The Clownfish, Regal tang and Idol are in the Reef Pack, the Starfish in Reef Pack II, and the Puffer is free.

## 2. The free freshwater tank

**Fish:** Neon, Guppy, Goldfish, Angelfish, Betta. **Water:** Reed. **Props:** Vallisneria, Plants, Pebble.

Everything here comes with the app: the whole Freshwater Pack in a cool green-teal water among long grasses. Bright little fish on green look like a real planted tank.

## 3. A koi pond

**Fish:** two Koi, Gold koi, Terrapin, Frog. **Water:** Pond. **Props:** Rock, Pebble, Plants.

Sunlit green water, a warm sandy bed, and koi drifting past a sleepy terrapin. A koi pond is a symbol of luck and perseverance in Japan and China, which our post on [what koi mean](/blog/koi-fish-meaning/) explains. Pond Pack and Pond Creatures Pack.

## 4. The midnight jellyfish lounge

**Fish:** three Jellyfish, Squid, Octopus. **Water:** Twilight. **Props:** Coral, Tube Sponge.

Twilight water falls from sapphire to an indigo that is nearly black, and jellyfish practically glow in it. A calm one for an evening Home Screen. Sea Creatures Pack.

## 5. Ocean giants

**Fish:** Blue whale, Humpback, Sperm whale, Orca, Dolphin. **Water:** Minimal or Twilight. **Props:** none, or a single rock.

The blue whale, the humpback and the sperm whale are drawn half again as big as ordinary fish, so give them the large widget and leave the bed almost empty: open water makes them look even bigger. Whale Pack.

## 6. Shark week

**Fish:** Great white, Hammer (the hammerhead), Thresher, Whale shark, Manta. **Water:** Twilight. **Props:** Coral Reef.

Sleek hunters and gentle giants in deep blue water. The whale shark, the biggest fish in the sea, is a gentle filter feeder that lives on plankton, so everyone is perfectly safe. Shark Pack.

## 7. The spooky tank

**Fish:** Ghost, Skeleton, Jellyfish, plus Robot and Submarine if you want company. **Water:** Pumpkin, or Dark. **Props:** Branches, Rock House.

Orange water, bare branches and a ghost drifting past a skeleton: ready for Halloween, or any gloomy Monday. In the Dark style the ghost seems to float in nothing at all. Special Pack, with the Jellyfish from the Sea Creatures Pack.

## 8. Cherry blossom

**Fish:** Betta, Guppy, Goldfish, Axolotl. **Water:** Blossom. **Props:** Pearl, Scallop, Conch.

Pale pink water with red and orange fish, and an axolotl smiling in the middle. Soft and bright, perfect next to a pink wallpaper. The Betta, Guppy and Goldfish are free; the Axolotl is in the Pond Creatures Pack.

## 9. A pineapple under the sea

**Fish:** Starfish, Squid, Puffer, Shrimp. **Water:** Minimal. **Props:** Pineapple House, Sponge, Rock House.

If you grew up with a certain yellow cartoon sponge, you'll know who lives in the pineapple house. Reef Pack II, Sea Creatures Pack and Freshwater Pack II.

## 10. One fish, one plant

**Fish:** a single Betta, or a single Goldfish. **Water:** Minimal. **Props:** Ferns.

Sometimes less is more: one fish with a name, one fern, and lots of water. A small widget with a single fish swimming is the calmest thing you can put on a Home Screen, and it costs nothing.

## 11. Pond life

**Fish:** Axolotl, Frog, Toad, Tadpole, Minnow. **Water:** Pond. **Props:** Plants, Rock.

Everything that hops, wriggles and lurks among the lily pads: the frog and the toad float rather than swim, the tadpole wriggles, and the axolotl smiles at all of it. A favourite with children. Pond Creatures Pack.

## 12. Calm lavender

**Fish:** Discus, Zebrafish, Rummynose, Gourami, Shrimp. **Water:** Lavender. **Props:** Vallisneria, Pebble.

Peaceful little fish from warm tropical rivers in pale lavender water, with the shrimp walking the bed between the pebbles. Freshwater Pack II.

## Make it yours, and share it

Once a tank looks right, keep it: **My Tanks** keeps as many designs per size as you like, so you can switch from the reef to the spooky tank in October and back again. The **Explore** page in the ⋯ menu has eighteen ready-made tanks to start from.

To show a friend, tap **⋯**, then **Share this tank**. Companions makes a picture of the tank and a code; a friend who opens the code gets your water and props on their shelf, with their own fish in it.

## Questions people ask

### How many fish can a tank hold?

Five per tank. Each widget size (small, medium and large) is its own tank with its own five, so you can keep fifteen fish across the three. More in [how many fish can I keep?](/help/how-many-fish/)

### Which of these tanks are free?

The free freshwater tank (2), one fish and one plant (10), and any tank built from the eight free fish: the Tang, Sardine and Puffer and the Freshwater Pack. Every water style and every prop is free too.

### Can I use the same fish twice?

Yes. Three clownfish are allowed; give them different names to tell them apart.
