iPhone widgets come in a few fixed sizes, measured in app icons. Knowing them makes it much easier to plan a Home Screen that looks tidy. Here is each size, what it's good for, what changed in iOS 27, and how to switch a widget from one size to another.

## The sizes at a glance

| Size | Space on the Home Screen | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Small | A square, 2 × 2 app icons | One thing at a glance: the weather, a photo, a small aquarium |
| Medium | A wide strip, 4 × 2 app icons | A list, a forecast, a wide tank of fish |
| Large | A big square, 4 × 4 app icons | A month, a news feed, a large tank with room for whales |
| Extra large | A whole page, 4 × 6 app icons (iOS 27) | A Home Screen page given to a single widget |

Not every app offers every size: each app chooses which sizes its widget comes in.

## Small

The most flexible size. Four fit in the space of a large widget, two sit side by side across the screen, and StandBy, the view your iPhone shows while charging on its side, uses small widgets, drawn large. A small widget shows one thing well: the time, the weather, a photo, or a tank with a few fish.

## Medium

The width of the screen and two rows of icons tall. Good for anything that reads left to right: a three-day forecast, your next few events, a wide strip of water with a school of fish crossing it.

## Large

The width of the screen and four rows tall: half a Home Screen. Room for a whole month's calendar or a big photo. In an aquarium it is the size for giants: in Companions, the blue whale, the humpback and the sperm whale are drawn half again as big as the other fish, and a large tank gives them space to cruise.

## Extra large: new in iOS 27

iOS 27, released in September 2026, brought the extra-large widget to iPhone. It fills a whole page of the Home Screen, four icons wide and six tall, in portrait. Calendar, Home, Music, News, Notes, Photos, Reminders, Shortcuts, Stocks, TV and Weather offer it, and other apps can add it too. It suits a Home Screen page given over to one thing, such as a big calendar or a photo frame, and especially the large screen of the foldable iPhone Duo.

## How to resize a widget

- **On iOS 27:** touch and hold the widget and choose a size from the menu.
- **On iOS 18 and later:** touch and hold the Home Screen until the icons jiggle, then drag the handle at a widget's bottom-right corner to change its size.
- **Or turn an app into a widget:** touch and hold an app's icon and tap one of the size icons in the menu.

If a size doesn't appear, that app doesn't offer it.

## Smart Stacks: several widgets in one space

Drag a widget onto another of the same size and they become a **Smart Stack**: one space you swipe through, up to ten widgets deep. Touch and hold the stack and tap **Edit Stack** to reorder it, and to turn **Smart Rotate** on (iOS shows the widget it thinks you need) or off (it stays where you leave it).

## Which size suits an aquarium?

Companions comes in small, medium and large, and each size is its own tank with its own five fish, so you can use more than one:

- **Small** for a little tank in a corner, and for StandBy at night.
- **Medium** for a wide reef or a pond with koi gliding across it.
- **Large** for whales and sharks, or a whole reef with a shelf of props.

In the app, swipe the main page sideways to see each size exactly as it will look on your Home Screen. [How to add an aquarium widget](/blog/add-the-widget/) has the steps.

## Lock Screen, Mac and iPad

- **Lock Screen widgets** are a different, smaller kind: circular, rectangular or a single line by the date, drawn in one tint.
- **On the Mac**, your iPhone's widgets can sit on the desktop in the same sizes, and macOS 27 adds the extra-large size there too. See [iPhone widgets on your Mac](/blog/mac-and-ipad/).
- **On the iPad**, the larger screen has had an extra-large widget for years.

## Questions people ask

### What are the widget sizes on iPhone?

Small (2 × 2 app icons), medium (4 × 2), large (4 × 4) and, since iOS 27, extra large (4 × 6, a whole page).

### How do I make a widget bigger on iPhone?

On iOS 27, touch and hold the widget and choose a size. Or touch and hold the Home Screen until the icons jiggle and drag the widget's corner handle.

### Why can't I find the extra-large widget?

You need iOS 27, and the app has to offer it. Calendar, Photos, Weather and several other Apple apps do.

### Which widget size is best?

It depends on what it shows: small for one glance, medium for a list or a wide picture, large for the thing you want to look at most.
