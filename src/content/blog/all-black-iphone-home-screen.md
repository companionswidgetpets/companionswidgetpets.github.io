An all-black Home Screen is the calmest way to use an iPhone: no bright colours pulling at you, just what you need, quietly. It also suits the iPhone's screen, where black pixels are simply switched off. Here is how to make one in about five minutes, and how to keep it from feeling lifeless.

## 1. Set a black wallpaper

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open <strong>Settings › Wallpaper</strong> and tap <strong>Add New Wallpaper</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>Colour</strong>, pick black and drag the slider all the way to the dark end.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Add</strong>, then use it for the Home Screen too.</li>
</ol>

A photo works just as well, if it's almost entirely black: a night sky, a dark texture or a black-and-white shot.

## 2. Turn on Dark Mode

**Settings › Display & Brightness › Dark.** Menus, Apple's apps and most widgets follow, going dark to match.

## 3. Make the icons dark

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Customise**, and choose **Dark**. App icons switch to their dark versions, with black backgrounds. For an even quieter look:

- Choose **Large** in the same panel: the icons grow and their names disappear.
- Or choose **Tinted** and drag the saturation all the way down, for icons in shades of grey.

## 4. Choose dark widgets

Most of Apple's widgets turn dark with Dark Mode: Clock, Calendar, Weather, Batteries. Keep to two or three, so the black stays the main thing.

Then add one with some life in it. In Companions, the **Dark** style was made for exactly this: water as black as the Home Screen allows, with the glass highlights turned down so the tank sits quietly among dark icons. Bright fish glow in it: the Neon, the Jellyfish, and the Ghost and the Skeleton from the Special Pack. Open the tank, tap **⋯**, then **Edit this tank**, and pick **Dark** from the style menu. In **Settings › App Icon**, the Dark icon matches.

## 5. Clear the clutter

- **Hide pages you don't need.** Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap the row of dots above the Dock, and untick the pages to hide. The apps stay in the App Library.
- **Leave space.** Apps and widgets can sit anywhere on the grid, so an empty row or two is allowed, and looks deliberate.
- **Keep the Dock to four apps** you use every day.

## 6. Finish the Lock Screen and the bedside

- Give the Lock Screen the same black wallpaper and a white clock.
- At night, StandBy shows widgets large while your iPhone charges on its side. A Dark Companions tank beside the clock is a lovely, dim thing to wake up to; our [StandBy guide](/blog/standby/) shows how.

## Does a black Home Screen save battery?

A little. Every current iPhone has an OLED screen, where black pixels are switched off rather than lit, so the more black on screen, the less power it draws. It won't double your battery life, but it doesn't hurt.

## Questions people ask

### How do I make my iPhone Home Screen black?

Set a black wallpaper (Settings › Wallpaper › Add New Wallpaper › Colour), turn on Dark Mode, then touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Customise, and choose Dark.

### How do I hide app names on my iPhone Home Screen?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Customise**, and choose **Large**: the icons grow and their names disappear.

### Are there dark widgets for iPhone?

Most Apple widgets go dark with Dark Mode. For something with life in it, the Companions aquarium has a Dark style made for black Home Screens, with fish that swim all day.
