Companions comes with an Apple Watch app that shows your small tank on your wrist: the same water, the same props, the same fish swimming the same way they do on your iPhone.

## Install the watch app

Open the **Watch** app on your iPhone and scroll down to **Available Apps**; tap **Install** beside Companions. If your watch is set to install apps automatically, it may already be there. The watch app appears in the watch's app list as Companions.

## What it shows

The watch shows the **small** tank in use: the one your small Home Screen widget shows. Fish, props and style all come across, drawn for the watch's screen with the rounded corners of its glass. The fish swim exactly as they do in the widget, because each fish's swim is set from its identity, not from the device.

Two things are different on the wrist:

- **Complications** in a tank corner, because the watch has a clock of its own and its screen is small.
- **Adding fish** happens on the iPhone and syncs across. Feeding works on the watch too: tap the knife and fork in the corner, then tap anywhere on the tank to drop food in.

## Choose the tank from your iPhone

In Companions on the iPhone, open **⋯ › Settings › Apple Watch**. The page draws your watch with the tank on its screen, as it will look. Swipe through the small tanks on your shelf to preview each one, and tap **Save and use tank** to make the one you're looking at the small tank in use, on the watch and on the Home Screen alike.

## How syncing works

The iPhone sends the tank to the watch over the connection the two already share, whenever the tank changes and the watch is reachable. Both devices need to be on and near each other, or on the same Wi-Fi network, for the change to go across; otherwise the watch keeps the last tank it was sent and catches up when they're back together.

## If something looks wrong

- **"No watch connected"** on the Settings page: check that the watch is paired to this iPhone and nearby, and that Companions is installed on it.
- **A fish is missing on the watch**: the watch app needs to know every species in the tank. If the watch app is older than the iPhone app, update it in the Watch app on your iPhone.
- **The watch shows an old tank**: open Companions on the iPhone with the watch nearby, and give it a moment.
