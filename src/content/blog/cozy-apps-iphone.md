A cozy app doesn't rush you. There is no timer running down, no score to beat and nobody waiting for your turn; just soft colours, small rituals and something pleasant to come back to. When the evenings draw in, these are the iPhone apps we reach for.

We make the first one, Companions. The others are favourites of ours, described from their App Store listings as of November 2026.

## Something to look at

### Companions

A little aquarium in a widget on your Home Screen, where hand-drawn fish swim all day with the app closed. You choose the fish, give them names and decorate their tank; then you simply look at it now and then, which turns out to be the point. Nothing needs feeding and nothing ever dies, so it is the one pet that never asks for anything on a busy day.

For a cozy look, try the warm green Pond water with koi and a frog, or Pumpkin orange with goldfish. At night, StandBy shows the tank beside the clock while your phone charges. The widget is free in every size, with eight fish.

## Games with no clock

### Stardew Valley

Inherit a run-down farm, plant crops, befriend the town, go fishing and slowly make a life. Probably the most loved cozy game there is, and on iPhone it is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases at all. (Apple Arcade members also get Stardew Valley+.)

### Unpacking

A story told entirely through boxes: you unpack someone's belongings across eight moves in their life, and work out who they are from what they keep. Its listing promises no timers, meters or scores. A one-time purchase.

### A Little to the Left

Tidying puzzles: straighten the pictures, sort the pencils, stack the plates, while a mischievous (but very cute) cat undoes your work. When a puzzle is too much, a "Let It Be" option lets you skip it. The first nine puzzles are free to try.

### Alba: A Wildlife Adventure

Spend a summer on a Mediterranean island, photographing its wildlife and helping protect it. Short, sunny and kind. On Apple Arcade.

### Neko Atsume 2

Put out food and toys in your garden and wait to see which cats come to visit. Every visitor goes in your Catbook. That's it, and it's perfect. Free, with ads and in-app purchases.

### Monument Valley 3

Guide a quiet traveller through impossible architecture that shifts as you turn it. Beautiful, gentle puzzles; the first two chapters are free, and one purchase unlocks the rest.

### Sky: Children of the Light

A softly lit world to wander, mostly about helping other players and making friends without words. Its listing's best line: share a bench at sunset. Free, with cosmetic purchases.

## With a Netflix membership

Netflix members can download these at no extra cost:

- **Cozy Grove: Camp Spirit**, a haunted island where you help friendly ghost bears, decorate a camp and, as its listing puts it, pet a dog (!).
- **Spiritfarer: Netflix Edition**, in which you ferry spirits on your boat and care for them before they move on. Gentle, and quietly moving.

## Small daily rituals

### Finch

A little pet bird who grows as you look after yourself: small self-care goals, a few kind words, a check-in on your mood. Free, with Finch Plus for extras.

### Merlin Bird ID

Made by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. Hold up your phone in the garden or on a walk and Merlin names the birds singing around you, by their song alone. Free, and a lovely way to start a morning.

## For bedtime

Two more for the end of the day: **Calm**, whose sleep stories include Bob Ross's "Happy Little Zzzs", and **Pixel Pals**, whose pixel pets can sit in StandBy so you can, in its listing's words, count sheep as you sleep.

## Make the phone itself feel cozy

1. **Warm the screen at night.** **Settings › Display & Brightness › Night Shift** shifts the colours warmer after dark, on a schedule.
2. **Choose a warm wallpaper**, cream, terracotta or forest green, and let it set the tone.
3. **Put something alive on the Home Screen.** A tank with Pond water and koi, or Pumpkin water and goldfish, looks like a lamp-lit corner.
4. **Turn the bedside into a fish tank.** StandBy shows the small Companions widget while your iPhone charges on its side; the Dark style was made for the night. Our [StandBy guide](/blog/standby/) shows how.

## Questions people ask

### What is a cozy game?

A game without pressure: no timers, no losing, no competition, usually with soft art and small, satisfying tasks like farming, tidying or decorating. Stardew Valley and Unpacking are classic examples.

### Are there cozy games on iPhone without in-app purchases?

Yes. Stardew Valley and Unpacking are one-time purchases, and Apple Arcade games like Alba have no in-app purchases at all.

### What are calming apps to look at?

Anything that moves slowly and asks nothing of you. A Companions tank on your Home Screen is exactly that, and [watching fish really is calming](/blog/why-watching-fish-is-relaxing/).
