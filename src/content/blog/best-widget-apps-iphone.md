The best widgets earn their space. You see your Home Screen a hundred times a day, so every square should either tell you something useful or make you glad you looked. These are the widget apps that do one or the other better than anything else, grouped by the job they do.

We make Companions, the first on the list. The others are apps we admire; facts about them come from their App Store listings as of November 2026.

## Something alive

### Companions: an aquarium that really swims

Most widgets are still pictures that iOS swaps every so often. The Companions widget is a fish tank where the fish swim continuously, turning, gliding and letting out the odd bubble, with the app closed and without draining your battery. You choose the fish from 58 hand-drawn species, give them names and decorate their tank.

It is also useful: with All Fish, a corner of the tank can show the time, the date, the weather, your step goal or the Moon phase, so one widget does two jobs. The widget is free in small, medium and large, with eight fish, and it works in StandBy, on Apple Watch, iPad and the Mac. [How the fish swim](/blog/how-the-widget-animates/) explains the trick.

### Pixel Pals: a pet that wanders

Pixel-art pets that live on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen, in StandBy and, best of all, in the Dynamic Island, where they trot about while you use other apps. The dog and the cat are free.

## Widgets you design yourself

### Widgetsmith

The classic widget builder: clocks, calendars, photos, weather, tides and more, in your own fonts and colours, in every size including extra large, and on the Lock Screen. Free, with a premium plan for weather and tides.

### Color Widgets

Simpler than Widgetsmith and very pretty: photo, clock and quote widgets in ready-made colour themes, for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen. Free, with a Pro plan.

### Widgy

For people who like to tinker: a layer-by-layer widget designer with dozens of visual effects, for the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and the Apple Watch. Free to try, with one-time unlocks for more widget slots.

## Weather

### CARROT Weather

Accurate forecasts with a personality: choose a voice from strictly professional to gleefully rude. Its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Live Activities and watch complications are some of the most detailed anywhere. Free to try, with a Premium subscription for the full set.

### Apple Weather

The one already on your phone, and still very good. A medium Weather widget is the most useful square most Home Screens have.

## Plan your day

### Fantastical

A calendar you can write to like a person ("dinner with Sam on Friday at 7") and a generous set of widgets. Free, with a Premium subscription.

### Things 3

The to-do app people love for its calm design, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and controls for Control Centre. A one-time purchase.

### Streaks

A habit tracker for up to 24 habits, with interactive widgets you can tick off right on the Home Screen, and a Live Activity for the one you're doing now. A one-time purchase, no subscription.

## People

### Locket Widget

Your closest friends' photos, delivered straight to a widget on your Home Screen moments after they take them. Free, with Locket Gold.

## Everything else

- **Shazam**: Apple's song-recognition app has Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that start listening with one tap.
- **Sky Guide**: a Moon widget and a Moon phase calendar on your Home Screen, from the stargazing app that names every star you point at.
- **Journal**: Apple's diary app has a widget with your streak and writing prompts that change through the day.
- **Batteries**: built into iOS, and the quickest way to see how charged your AirPods are.

## How to build a Home Screen of widgets that works

1. **One big widget you love.** Put it at the top: a medium or large tank, a photo, a calendar.
2. **Two or three you actually use.** Weather, calendar, a to-do list.
3. **Stack the rest.** Drag one widget onto another of the same size to make a stack, then swipe through it, so the screen stays tidy.
4. **Match the colours.** A wallpaper, tinted icons and widgets in the same palette look calm rather than busy. See our [aesthetic Home Screen ideas](/blog/aesthetic-iphone-home-screen-ideas/).

## Questions people ask

### What is the best widget app for iPhone?

For something to enjoy looking at, Companions: a fish tank whose fish really swim on your Home Screen, free in every size. To design your own widgets, Widgetsmith. For weather, CARROT Weather.

### Are widget apps free?

Most are free to download. Some keep their best widgets behind a subscription; Companions' widget is free in every size, with eight fish, every water style and every prop.

### Do widgets drain the battery?

Not much. iOS draws widgets itself and updates them on a budget, so a widget costs far less than an open app. Companions' fish swim using the same animation iOS uses for a clock widget's hands, with nothing running in the background: see [does Companions drain my battery?](/help/battery/)

### What widget sizes does iPhone have?

Small, medium and large, plus an extra-large size on newer versions of iOS. Our guide to [iPhone widget sizes](/blog/iphone-widget-sizes/) shows which fits what.
