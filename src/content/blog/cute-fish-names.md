A fish with a name stops being "the orange one" and becomes somebody. Whether it lives in a real tank on your desk or in a widget on your Home Screen, the right name makes you smile every time you see it. Here are 150 ideas, sorted so you can find one that fits.

## How to choose a good fish name

- **Look at it.** Colour is the easiest start: an orange fish is a Mango, a black one a Pepper, a blue one a Sky.
- **Watch how it swims.** A fish that darts about is a Zoom; one that hangs in the corner thinking about life is a Gerald.
- **Say it out loud.** Two syllables are easy to call; one is easy to shout across the room.
- **Pick a theme for the whole tank.** Five fish named after five spices, five planets or five members of a band is funnier than five unrelated names.

## Fish names by species

**Goldfish:** Goldie, Midas, Nugget, Bullion, Penny, Klondike, Marigold, Cheddar. A goldfish deserves a name that sounds rich.

**Betta:** bettas have flowing fins and a big personality, so give them a stage name. Duchess, Valentino, Gatsby, Diva, Rhapsody, Merlot, Sapphire, Ruby, Kimono, Tutu.

**Clownfish:** Nemo is taken by a few million fish already. Try Marlin, Giggles, Chuckles, Pickles, Stripes, Popcorn or Tangerine.

**Koi:** Japanese names suit them. Sakura (cherry blossom), Hana (flower), Kin (gold), Gin (silver), Yuki (snow), Momo (peach), Sora (sky), Ryu (dragon), or Kohaku, after the red-and-white variety. Our post on [what koi mean](/blog/koi-fish-meaning/) explains why a koi is good luck.

**Pufferfish:** Puff, Puffball, Spike, Balloon, Pokey, Mochi, Popcorn.

**Sharks:** Bruce, Jaws, Chomp, Gnasher, Razor, Meg (short for megalodon), or Nibbles, for irony.

**Whales and dolphins:** Moby, Willy, Humphrey (after the humpback that swam into San Francisco Bay in 1985), Splash, Echo, Atlas, Big Bertha. A black-and-white orca is an obvious Oreo.

**Octopus, squid and jellyfish:** Inky (after a famous escape artist from New Zealand's National Aquarium), Squiggles, Wiggles, Wobble, Octavia, Jelly, Ink.

**Turtles:** Shelly, Sheldon, Tank, Speedy, Crush, Pickle.

**Seahorses:** Pony, Trotter, Pegasus, Seabiscuit.

**Axolotls:** Axl, Lotl, Bubblegum, Gummy, Smiley, Wasabi. [Our axolotl facts](/blog/axolotl-facts/) may inspire more.

## Questions people ask

### What is a good name for a fish?

Something short that matches how it looks or moves: Mango for an orange fish, Zoom for a fast one, Mochi for a round one. Puns like Gill Murray or Fin Diesel are a favourite for a reason.

### What should I name my goldfish?

Gold names suit goldfish: Goldie, Midas, Nugget, Penny, Marigold or Butterscotch. For a pair, try Fish and Chips.

### What are good names for two fish?

Pairs that belong together: Salt and Pepper, Ping and Pong, Sushi and Wasabi, Peanut Butter and Jelly.

### Do fish know their names?

Not the way a dog does. But many fish learn to recognise the person who feeds them and come to the front of the tank, and in experiments archerfish have even learned to tell human faces apart. The name is mostly for you, which is fine.
