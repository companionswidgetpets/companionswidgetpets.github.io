A tank in Companions is a design: a water style, props standing on the bed, and perhaps a complication in a corner. Fish are kept separately, per widget size, so you can change the whole look of a tank and the same fish swim on in it.

## Where tanks live: My Tanks

Tap **⋯** at the top of the main page and choose **My Tanks**. The page has a shelf of tanks for each widget size; swipe sideways to move between small, medium and large, as on the main page. One tank per shelf is **in use**, and that is the one the widget of that size shows.

- Tap a tank on the shelf to see it large, with your fish resting in it, and **Use this tank** to switch to it.
- Tap the dashed **+** to start a new, empty tank.
- Touch and hold a tank for **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. Duplicate is the way to try a variation without losing the original.
- The **Edit** button opens the Edit Tank page for the tank you're looking at.

## Ready-made tanks: Explore

**⋯ › Explore** lists eighteen ready-made tanks, six for each size, in the six older styles. Tap **Add** and a copy goes on your shelf, ready to use or to change; the original stays on the Explore page, so you can add it again later.

## The Edit Tank page

Open it with **⋯ › Edit this tank** for the tank in use, or from My Tanks. It is a card that rises over the page, with the tank at the top, drawn at the size of that widget.

### Water

The **style menu** at the top shows the eight waters as swatches: Minimal, Twilight, Pond, Reed, Blossom, Dark, Pumpkin and Lavender. Tap one and the tank changes at once. [The eight tank styles](/styles/) shows each in full.

### Props

Tap **Add prop** and choose from the nineteen: Coral, Ferns, Sponge, Branches, Plants, Vallisneria, Rock, Pebble, Murex Shell, Scallop, Pearl, Conch, Coral Reef, Anemone, Brain Coral, Tube Sponge, Pineapple House, Moai House and Rock House. A prop drops onto the bed, a little to the left; from there:

- **Drag** it anywhere. Props stand with their feet on the bed line, the same line the starfish and the shrimp live on.
- **Resize** with two fingers, or by dragging a corner of the box around the selected prop. The large tank has room for bigger props than the small one.
- Tap a prop to select it, then **Flip** to mirror it or **Remove** to take it out.
- Props are drawn from the back of the tank to the front in the order you added them, so add the background first.

A prop can be in a tank several times over, and props are placed as fractions of the glass, so a tank arranged on the iPhone looks the same in the widget and on an iPad.

### Undo and redo

The two arrows beside the style menu undo and redo; one touch is one step, so a long drag is a single undo. On an iPad with a keyboard, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z work too.

### Complications

Under the actions, **Complications** adds the time, the date, the weather, your steps or the Moon to a corner. They come with All Fish; see [the complications guide](/blog/complications/).

### Saving

There is no Save button. Tap **Close**, or swipe the card down, and the tank is saved as it stands; the widget updates to match.

## Sharing a design

**⋯ › Share this tank** makes a picture of the tank and a code that carries its design. Anyone with Companions who opens the code gets the tank on their shelf, with their own fish in it. More in [the little things](/blog/little-things/).
