A phone gets boring when it becomes only a tool: the same grid of icons, the same feeds, the same everything. The fix isn't more apps. It's a few things that surprise you, teach you something or make you smile when you pick it up. Here are twelve, from apps to settings you may never have touched.

We make the first app here, Companions. The rest are other people's apps we love, described from their App Store listings as of November 2026.

## Apps that make your phone feel alive

### 1. Companions: fish on your Home Screen

The quickest cure for a boring Home Screen is something alive on it. Companions puts a little aquarium in a widget, and the fish really swim, all day, with the app closed. You pick them from 58 hand-drawn species (including a ghost, a robot and a tiny submarine, for when fish feel too sensible), give them names, and decorate the tank. Some mornings one of them says hello. The widget is free in every size.

### 2. Pixel Pals: a pet in the Dynamic Island

A pixel-art dog or cat that lives in the Dynamic Island and trots across the top of your screen while you use other apps. Also on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and in StandBy. Free, with more animals in Premium.

### 3. Locket Widget: your friends' photos on your Home Screen

Friends send a photo and, moments later, it appears in a widget on your Home Screen. A small, constant reminder of the people you like most.

## Apps that make the world more interesting

### 4. Merlin Bird ID: name the birds around you

From the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, free. Hold up your phone outside and Merlin names the birds singing nearby by their song alone, even the ones you can't see. A walk in the park becomes a treasure hunt.

### 5. Sky Guide: a map of the stars

Point your iPhone at the night sky and Sky Guide labels the stars, planets and satellites in front of you. It also has a Moon widget for your Home Screen.

### 6. Seek by iNaturalist: identify anything alive

Point the camera at a plant, a mushroom or an animal and Seek tells you what it is, and gives you badges for the species you find. Free, and brilliant with children.

### 7. Shazam: lyrics that sing along

Everyone knows Shazam names songs. Fewer know it shows the lyrics live, line by line, as the song plays, karaoke-style.

### 8. CARROT Weather: a forecast with attitude

Weather that talks back. Choose a personality from strictly professional to gleefully rude, and every forecast comes with a comment. Free to try, with a Premium subscription.

## Built-in tricks you may have missed

### 9. Give your icons a new look

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Customise**. Choose **Dark**, **Clear** or **Tinted**, in any colour, and the whole Home Screen changes in a second. Choose **Large** to hide the app names. Our [aesthetic Home Screen ideas](/blog/aesthetic-iphone-home-screen-ideas/) has eight looks to copy.

### 10. A Lock Screen that changes

On the Lock Screen, touch and hold, tap **+**, and choose **Photo Shuffle** for a new photo every time you lock your phone, every hour or every day. A **spatial scene** gives a photo a 3D look that moves as you tilt the phone.

### 11. A bedside display

Put your iPhone on a charger, on its side, and StandBy turns it into a bedside clock with widgets. A swimming tank beside the clock is a good way to wake up. Here are [the best StandBy widgets](/blog/best-standby-widgets/).

### 12. Back Tap

**Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap** lets a double or triple tap on the back of your iPhone do something: take a screenshot, turn on the torch, or run any shortcut you like. It feels like a secret button.

## The bonus: less, not more

Sometimes the most refreshing change is taking things away. Move the apps that pull you in to the second page, keep the first one for things that make you feel good, and let a tank of fish take up the space where the feeds used to be.

## Questions people ask

### How can I make my iPhone more fun?

Put something alive on the Home Screen, like the Companions aquarium or a Pixel Pals pet, change the icon look under Edit, then Customise, and try apps that make the world more interesting, like Merlin Bird ID and Sky Guide.

### How do I customise my iPhone Home Screen?

Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Customise** to change the icon look, or **Add Widget** to add widgets. Change the wallpaper in **Settings › Wallpaper**.

### What are cool widgets for iPhone?

An aquarium where the fish really swim (Companions), a pet in the Dynamic Island (Pixel Pals), your friends' photos (Locket) and the Moon phase (Sky Guide). More in [the best widget apps for iPhone](/blog/best-widget-apps-iphone/).
