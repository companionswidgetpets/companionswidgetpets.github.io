With its feathery pink frills and permanent smile, the axolotl may be the most loved creature in any aquarium. It's also one of the strangest. Here are fifteen facts about the salamander that never grows up.

## What an axolotl is

1. **It isn't a fish.** It's often called the Mexican walking fish, but an axolotl is an amphibian: a salamander, related to frogs and newts.
2. **It never grows up.** Most salamanders start life in water and change into land animals with lungs. Axolotls skip the change and keep their baby features, the feathery gills and the fin along the tail, for their whole adult life. Scientists call this neoteny.
3. **The frills are gills.** Those six feathery stalks on its head are external gills, and the more feathery they are, the happier the water. Axolotls have lungs too, and sometimes swim up to gulp air.
4. **The smile is just the shape of its mouth**, but nobody minds.

## The superpower

5. **It can regrow almost anything.** An axolotl can regrow a lost leg, its tail, parts of its spinal cord and heart, and even parts of its brain, without a scar. That's why scientists around the world study it.
6. **Its genome is enormous.** The axolotl's DNA is around ten times longer than ours, one of the largest animal genomes ever sequenced.

## Where axolotls live

7. **In one place only.** Wild axolotls live in the canals of Xochimilco, in the south of Mexico City, the last remains of a great lake system. They once lived in nearby Lake Chalco too, which has since been drained.
8. **They are critically endangered.** Pollution, introduced fish and a growing city have made wild axolotls very rare. Conservation projects work with local farmers to keep clean refuges in the canals.
9. **They like it cold.** Axolotls are happiest in cool water, around 16 to 18 °C (60 to 64 °F), and warm water stresses them.

## Axolotls and people

10. **The name is Aztec.** "Axolotl" comes from Nahuatl, the language of the Aztecs: atl means water. Legend links it to Xolotl, the god of fire and lightning, who turned himself into an axolotl to escape being sacrificed.
11. **It's on Mexico's money.** The 50-peso banknote, introduced in 2021, shows an axolotl among the canals of Xochimilco.
12. **Wild axolotls aren't pink.** In the wild they are dark olive or brown with gold flecks, for camouflage. The pink ones, called leucistic, are bred in captivity, along with gold, white and black varieties.
13. **They live a long time.** Ten to fifteen years in good care is normal.
14. **They hunt by sucking.** Axolotls eat worms, insects and small fish, opening their mouths so fast that the food is pulled in with the water.
15. **They aren't legal pets everywhere.** Some places, including California, don't allow them to be kept at home, to protect wild amphibians. Check your local rules before buying one.

## Questions people ask

### Is an axolotl a fish?

No. Despite the nickname "Mexican walking fish", an axolotl is a salamander, an amphibian like a frog or a newt.

### Why do axolotls smile?

They don't mean to: the upturned shape of their mouth just looks like a smile.

### Are axolotls endangered?

Yes. Wild axolotls are critically endangered, and live only in the canals of Xochimilco in Mexico City.

### Can axolotls regrow limbs?

Yes, and much more: legs, tails, parts of the spinal cord and heart, and even parts of the brain.
