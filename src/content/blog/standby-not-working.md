StandBy is the full-screen view an iPhone shows when it's charging on its side: a big clock, your photos or two widgets. When it doesn't appear, it's almost always one of four conditions not being met. Here is what StandBy needs, and what to try when it misbehaves.

## What StandBy needs

All four, at the same time:

1. **StandBy turned on**, in **Settings › StandBy**.
2. **The iPhone charging**, on a cable, a MagSafe charger or any other wireless charger.
3. **On its side**, in landscape, and **still**: propped on a stand or leaning, not in your hand.
4. **Locked.** Lock it with the side button and wait a moment.

(The foldable iPhone Duo is the exception: it shows StandBy partly folded, like a tent or a book, without a charger.)

## If StandBy doesn't appear at all

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open <strong>Settings › StandBy</strong> and check that <strong>StandBy</strong> is on.</li>
<li>Check the iPhone is really charging: the battery icon shows a bolt. Try another cable or charger.</li>
<li>Turn the iPhone fully on its side and leave it still for a few seconds. A slight tilt or a wobbly stand can stop it.</li>
<li>Lock the iPhone with the side button, then wait.</li>
<li>Restart the iPhone, and update iOS in <strong>Settings › General › Software Update</strong>.</li>
</ol>

StandBy needs iOS 17 or later, so every iPhone that runs iOS 26 or iOS 27 has it.

## If StandBy keeps turning off

That depends on your iPhone.

- **Always-on iPhones** (iPhone 14 Pro and later Pro models, iPhone 17, iPhone Air and iPhone Duo) can keep StandBy visible all night. In **Settings › StandBy**, tap **Display** and choose when the screen turns off: **Automatically**, **After 20 Seconds** or **Never**. **Low Power Mode** turns the always-on display off, so StandBy goes dark with it: turn Low Power Mode off while you charge.
- **Other iPhones** turn the screen off after a short while, as they do on the Lock Screen. That's normal. Tap the screen, nudge the table or speak to Siri and StandBy comes back.

On always-on iPhones, iOS also dims StandBy further when the room is dark and nothing has moved for a while. It brightens again when you move or tap.

## If StandBy is red

That's **Night Mode**: in a dark room StandBy dims and tints everything red, so it won't light up the bedroom. To keep the colours, turn **Night Mode** off in **Settings › StandBy**. If you'd like a dark screen with colour, use dark widgets instead, such as the Companions tank in the Dark style.

## If a widget won't show in StandBy

- StandBy uses each app's **small** widget, shown large. An app needs a small widget that supports StandBy; when you tap **+** in StandBy's widget view, iOS lists the ones that do.
- Open the app once after installing it, so iOS knows about its widgets.
- If the widget is blank or out of date, remove it from the stack (touch and hold, tap the minus) and add it again.
- **Smart Rotate** swaps widgets in a stack through the day. To keep one in view, touch and hold the stack, tap **Edit Stack** and turn **Smart Rotate** off.

## Questions people ask

### Why is my StandBy not showing?

StandBy needs four things at once: StandBy turned on in Settings, the iPhone charging, lying still on its side, and locked. If one is missing, it won't appear.

### Why does StandBy turn off after a few seconds?

Only some iPhones keep StandBy on: the always-on models (iPhone 14 Pro and later Pro models, iPhone 17, iPhone Air and iPhone Duo). Others turn the screen off after a while; tap it to bring StandBy back. On always-on models, check that Low Power Mode is off.

### How do I turn off the red light in StandBy?

Turn off **Night Mode** in **Settings › StandBy**.

### Does StandBy work with any charger?

Yes. A cable, MagSafe or any other wireless charger, as long as the iPhone is charging and lying on its side.
