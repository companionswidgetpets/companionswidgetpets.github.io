Companions is deliberately undemanding: nothing needs you. But there is a lot to do if you feel like it.

## Feeding

On the main page, tap the **fork and knife** at the top of the tank. Pinch above the water with two fingers and let go: flakes sprinkle down, and the fish turn and go after them, each at its own pace, until the food is gone. A fish that has just swallowed lets a few small bubbles out. Tap the button again when you're done, or just leave; nobody goes hungry, and nothing is kept score of.

## Bubbles

Tap the glass anywhere and a puff of bubbles rises from under your finger, swaying as it climbs, flattening against the surface and bursting with a little ring. The fish let out the odd small bubble as they swim, too. Bubbles are the one prop you don't place: the tank makes its own.

## Greetings

Some mornings, afternoons and evenings, one of your fish says hello as the app opens: a short greeting in a thought bubble over the tray, there for a few seconds and gone. Some afternoons it is just bubbles. Tap the bubble to pop it. If you'd like a fish to speak up, the Developer section in Settings has a **Say hello** button on test builds; in the shipping app it is left to chance, about half the time, once per part of the day.

## Names

Every fish starts out named for its species. Tap one in the tray to rename it: a friend, a family member, a cat you miss, the cast of a sitcom. Names show in the tray, and VoiceOver reads them from the widget. Two fish of the same species can be told apart only by their names, which is the point. Names stay on your device; see the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Sharing a tank

**⋯ › Share this tank** makes two things and opens the share sheet:

- A **picture**: the tank on a card with the app's name, ready for Messages or a story.
- A **code** that carries the tank's design, its style, props and complication. Someone with Companions who opens it gets your tank on their shelf, with their own fish in it. Nothing about you or your fish travels with it.

You can also save the code's QR to Photos.

## Rating and sharing the app

**Settings** has **Share Companions**, which opens the share sheet with a link to the app, and **Rate Companions**, which opens the App Store's rating form. Both are the best thing anyone can do for a small app, and both are optional.

## The app icon

**Settings › App Icon** offers six icons to match your tank: Minimal, Twilight, Pond, Reed, Blossom and Dark. The change takes a moment and iOS confirms it.

## Speed, size and language

Also in Settings: **Swim Speed** (five notches, a quarter slower to a quarter faster), **Fish Size** (five notches, 75% to 125%), **Temperature** for the weather complication, and **Language**, which switches the app between its twelve languages without changing your phone's.

## First-time notes

The first time you open the app, a few short notes point out the ⋯ menu and the +. They go away once you've used them, and don't come back.
