Companions is a widget first and an app second, so this is the step that matters.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open Companions once, so it has a tank to show. A fresh install starts with a small Minimal tank and three fish.</li>
<li>Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen until the icons start to jiggle.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Edit</strong> in the top-left corner, then <strong>Add Widget</strong>.</li>
<li>Search for <strong>Companions</strong>, or scroll to it.</li>
<li>Swipe between the three sizes: small (a square), medium (a wide tank) and large (a tall one). Tap <strong>Add Widget</strong>.</li>
<li>Drag it where you like and tap <strong>Done</strong>.</li>
</ol>

The widget shows exactly what the app shows for that size: the same water, props and fish. You can add more than one, in the same or different sizes, and each size keeps its own set of fish.

If the widget appears empty or shows an older tank, open the app once; it tells iOS to redraw its widgets whenever something changes.
