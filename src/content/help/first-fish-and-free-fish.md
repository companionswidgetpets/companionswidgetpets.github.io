Companions is free to download, and eight of its fish are free to keep:

- **The stock fish**, the ones every tank starts with: the Tang, the Sardine and the Puffer.
- **The Freshwater Pack**, a free taste of the rest: the Neon, the Guppy, the Goldfish, the Angelfish and the Betta.

A fresh install puts the three stock fish in the small tank, so there is something swimming in your widget straight away. Add the freshwater fish from the **+** in the tray, under **Freshwater Pack**.

The other packs are unlocked in the app: the Reef Pack, Pond Pack, Sea Creatures Pack, Pond Creatures Pack, Shark Pack, Whale Pack, Reef Pack II, Freshwater Pack II, Open Ocean Pack and Special Pack, each a one-time purchase, or all of them at once with **All Fish**.
