Companions makes the three Home Screen widgets: small, medium and large, in full colour. The Lock Screen uses a different kind of widget, small and drawn in a single colour, so a tank there would be a grey outline of fish rather than the fish themselves. We have chosen not to make one for now.

Two places come close:

- **StandBy** shows the small widget full-colour on its side while your iPhone charges, so the tank is the first thing you see when you look over. See [Does Companions work in StandBy?](/help/standby/)
- **Apple Watch** shows your small tank in the Companions watch app. See [Does Companions work on Apple Watch?](/help/apple-watch/)

If a Lock Screen tank matters to you, [email us](mailto:companionswidgetpets@gmail.com); the requests we hear most are the ones we build.
