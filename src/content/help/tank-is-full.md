Each tank holds five fish, so that each one has a name and a place of its own in the tray. When all five slots are taken, the **+** at the end of the tray dims, and tapping it gives a little nudge and the message **Tank is full**.

To add another fish:

- **Make room**: tap a fish in the tray and choose **Remove**. It dissolves into the water and its slot opens.
- **Use another tank**: swipe the main page sideways to the medium or large tank. Each widget size has its own five fish, so there is room for fifteen fish across the three.

The limit is the same for every tank, free or not; it is what keeps a small widget readable.
