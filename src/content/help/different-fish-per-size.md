Yes. Each widget size is its own tank, and since Companions 4.5 each has its own fish as well. Swipe the main page sideways to the medium or large tank and the tray under it shows that tank's fish; add and remove fish there as usual.

That means a Home Screen can hold, say, a small blue reef tank with a clownfish and a tang, and a wide green pond tank with koi and a terrapin, each with five fish of its own and each showing its own water and props.

Fish don't move between sizes on their own. To have the same fish in two sizes, add it to each. The stock fish a fresh install starts with (the Tang, Sardine and Puffer) are in the small tank only; the medium and large tanks start empty, ready for whatever you choose.

Tanks themselves are kept per size too: the **My Tanks** page has a shelf for each size, with one tank in use at a time, and the Explore page's ready-made tanks come in all three sizes.
