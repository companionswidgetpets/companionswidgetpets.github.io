Yes. Tap **⋯** at the top of the main page, then **Edit this tank**. Under the actions, tap **Add prop** and choose from the nineteen: Coral, Ferns, Sponge, Branches, Plants, Vallisneria, Rock, Pebble, Murex Shell, Scallop, Pearl, Conch, Coral Reef, Anemone, Brain Coral, Tube Sponge, Pineapple House, Moai House and Rock House.

On the page:

- **Drag** a prop to move it. Props stand on the bed, where the fish that keep to the bed (the starfish and the shrimp) also live.
- **Pinch** or drag a corner to resize it.
- Tap a prop and use the buttons to **flip** it or **remove** it.
- **Undo** and **Redo** are beside the style menu; every touch is one step.
- Tap **Close** when you're done. The tank saves itself.

A prop can appear more than once, so two ferns and three rocks are fine. Props are placed as a fraction of the glass, so an arrangement looks the same in the widget as in the app. Each tank has its own arrangement, and the **Explore** page has eighteen ready-made tanks to start from.
