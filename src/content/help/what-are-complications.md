Borrowed from watch faces, a complication is a small, useful thing in a corner of the glass. Companions has five:

- **Time**: the time of day, in your device's own style.
- **Date**: the weekday, day and month.
- **Weather**: the conditions and temperature where you are, under a symbol for the weather. It needs your location once, and fetches a forecast three times a day so the widget can follow the weather hour by hour.
- **Step Goal**: today's steps from Health, with a bar showing how far towards your goal you've walked.
- **Moon Phase**: tonight's Moon, drawn and named, the right way up for your hemisphere.

A tank has one complication, in whichever corner you choose. Set it on the **Edit Tank** page, under **Complications**; it is part of the tank's design, so it shows wherever that tank is shown, in the app and in the widget, and travels with the tank when you share it. The Apple Watch leaves it off, since the watch has a clock of its own.

Complications come with **All Fish**, lifetime or monthly. The tap on a locked complication opens the unlock sheet.
