A tank holds five fish, so each one gets a name and a place of its own. The tray under the tank has six slots: five for fish and one for the **+** that adds the next one. When the tank is full, the + dims, and tapping it says so.

Each widget size is its own tank, with its own fish. Swipe the main page sideways to the medium or large tank and the tray follows, showing that tank's fish. So you can keep up to fifteen fish altogether: five small, five medium, five large.

The fish you add don't have to be different species. Three clownfish are allowed, and the app tells them apart by name.

To make room, tap a fish in the tray and choose **Remove**; it dissolves into the water. Removing a fish deletes it from your device, and nothing else happens: no fish ever dies on its own.
