Yes. Since macOS Sonoma, a Mac can show the widgets of an iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account, as long as the two are near each other or on the same Wi-Fi. Companions doesn't need a Mac app for that.

<ol class="steps">
<li>On the Mac, open <strong>System Settings › Desktop &amp; Dock</strong>, scroll to <strong>Widgets</strong>, and turn on <strong>Use iPhone Widgets</strong>.</li>
<li>Right-click (or Control-click) the desktop and choose <strong>Edit Widgets</strong>.</li>
<li>Find <strong>Companions</strong> in the list on the left; iPhone widgets are marked with a small phone. Drag a size onto the desktop, or into Notification Centre.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Done</strong>.</li>
</ol>

The Mac shows the widget as your iPhone draws it, so changes you make on the iPhone appear on the Mac. How lively the fish look there depends on how often macOS refreshes an iPhone widget, which Apple doesn't let apps control; on the Home Screen and in StandBy they always swim.

Companions also runs natively on iPad, with widgets of its own: see [Does Companions work on iPad?](/help/ipad/)
