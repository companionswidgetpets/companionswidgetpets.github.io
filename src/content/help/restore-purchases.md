Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, and the app checks them with the App Store. If one isn't showing:

<ol class="steps">
<li>Check you are signed in to the App Store with the account that bought the pack: <strong>Settings › Apple Account</strong> on your iPhone.</li>
<li>In Companions, open <strong>⋯ › Settings</strong> and tap <strong>Restore Purchases</strong>.</li>
<li>If you bought the pack moments ago and it hasn't appeared, wait a minute; some purchases, such as those needing a parent's approval with Ask to Buy, stay pending until Apple confirms them.</li>
</ol>

On a new device or after reinstalling, Restore Purchases is all it takes. Fish and tanks themselves aren't restored by this; they are kept on each device.

If a purchase went wrong, refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). For anything else, [email us](mailto:companionswidgetpets@gmail.com) and we'll help.
