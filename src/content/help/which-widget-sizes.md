Companions offers the three Home Screen sizes iOS has for every app:

- **Small**: a square, the size of four app icons. It also fits in StandBy.
- **Medium**: a wide tank, the width of the screen and the height of two rows of icons.
- **Large**: the width of the screen and four rows tall, with room for the big fish and a shelf of props.

In the app, the main page is three pages: swipe sideways to move between the small, medium and large tank. What you see on each page is what that widget size will look like on your Home Screen, to the pixel, including the glass highlight iOS lays over every widget.

Since Companions 4.5, each size keeps its own set of fish. A small reef tank and a large pond tank can share a Home Screen, each with fish that suit it.

Companions doesn't offer Lock Screen widgets, which use a different, monochrome format; see [Can I put the aquarium on my Lock Screen?](/help/lock-screen-widget/)
