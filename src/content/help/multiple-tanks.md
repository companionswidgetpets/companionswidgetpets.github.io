Yes. Tap **⋯** at the top of the main page and choose **My Tanks**. The page has a shelf for each widget size; swipe sideways to move between small, medium and large, the same way the main page does.

Each shelf holds as many tanks as you like, each with its own style, props and complication. One is **in use**, and that is the one the widget of that size shows. Tap another to look at it in the big preview, and **Use this tank** to switch.

- Tap the dashed **+** to start a new tank.
- Touch and hold a tank for **Edit**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**.
- **Edit** opens the Edit Tank page for that tank.

Fish are not part of a tank design: they belong to the size. Switch the small widget from a Blossom tank to a Dark one and the same five fish swim on in the new water. That way a shelf of tanks is a wardrobe for the same fish rather than a set of separate aquariums.

The **Explore** page, also in the ⋯ menu, has eighteen ready-made tanks, six per size, that you can add to a shelf with one tap and then make your own.
