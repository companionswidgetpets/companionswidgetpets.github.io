Companions saves your tank in a storage area the widget reads, and asks iOS to redraw the widget whenever you change something. Almost always the widget follows within a second or two. When it doesn't:

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open Companions, swipe to the tank size the widget shows, and go back to the Home Screen. Opening the app refreshes the widgets.</li>
<li>Check that the widget is showing the tank you think it is. Each size has its own tank and its own fish; the small widget shows the small tank, not the medium one.</li>
<li>If the widget looks frozen or empty, touch and hold it, choose <strong>Remove Widget</strong>, then add it again.</li>
<li>Restart your iPhone. iOS occasionally keeps an old snapshot of a widget until it restarts.</li>
</ol>

If a tank you received by code or from Explore shows without its complication, that is expected: the time, date, weather, steps and Moon come with All Fish.
