No. Companions is deliberately the easy kind of pet. Your fish don't get hungry, they don't get sick, the water never needs changing and nothing ever dies. Leave the app for a month and the tank is exactly as you left it.

Feeding exists because it's fun, not because it's needed. On the main page, tap the **fork and knife** button at the top of the tank. Pinch the food over the water and let go: flakes drift down, and the fish turn and go after them. When you're done, tap the button again or just leave; the fish carry on as before.

A few other things happen only because they're nice:

- **Tap the glass** and a puff of bubbles rises from under your finger.
- Fish breathe out the odd small bubble as they swim.
- Some mornings and afternoons, a fish greets you in a little thought bubble as the app opens. Tap it to pop it.

None of it is kept score of, and none of it is ever asked of you.
