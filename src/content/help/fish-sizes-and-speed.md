question: "Can I make the fish bigger, smaller, faster or slower?"
summary: "Yes. Settings has a Fish Size control with five steps from 75% to 125% and a Swim Speed control from a quarter slower to a quarter faster. Both apply to the app and the widgets alike."

Open **Settings** from the **⋯** menu at the top of the main page.

**Fish Size** has five steps: 75%, 90%, 100%, 110% and 125% of the standard size. New tanks start at 90%, a nudge under standard, which suits the glass. Every species keeps its own proportion: whales are drawn half again as big as sardines at every setting, and the chunkier small fish a touch smaller.

**Swim Speed** has five steps too, from a quarter slower to a quarter faster than the pace the fish have always swum at. New tanks start one notch under normal. A fish takes the same path at every speed, just quicker or slower along it.

Both settings are shared with the widgets, so the Home Screen and the app always agree. They change how the fish are drawn in the tank; the tray and the sheets always show every species at one size.
