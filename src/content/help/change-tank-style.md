A tank's water is its **style**. There are eight: Minimal (blue), Twilight (deep blue to indigo), Pond (green over sand), Reed (teal over tan), Blossom (pink), Dark (black, for all-black Home Screens), Pumpkin (orange) and Lavender.

<ol class="steps">
<li>On the main page, swipe to the tank you want to change: each widget size is its own tank.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>⋯</strong> at the top, then <strong>Edit this tank</strong>.</li>
<li>Open the style menu at the top of the Edit Tank page and tap a style. The water changes under your finger.</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Close</strong>. The tank saves itself and the widget updates.</li>
</ol>

Styles belong to tanks, so your small and large tanks can be different colours. The page behind the tank takes on a pale wash of the water, and the Share button its hue.

If you'd like the app icon to match, **Settings › App Icon** has six: Minimal, Twilight, Pond, Reed, Blossom and Dark. See [the eight tank styles](/styles/) for what each looks like and which fish suit it.
