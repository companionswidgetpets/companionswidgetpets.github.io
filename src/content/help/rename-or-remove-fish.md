The tray under the tank shows the fish in it, with their names. Tap one:

- **Rename**: change the name and tap Done. A name can be anything: your best friend, a K-pop star, the cast of a sitcom. It shows in the tray and when VoiceOver reads the widget; nothing is drawn on the fish itself.
- **Remove**: the fish dissolves into the water and its slot opens for another. Removing deletes the fish from your device.

Every fish starts with its species as its name, so the clownfish is "Clownfish" until you say otherwise. Two fish of the same species can have different names, which is how the app tells them apart.

Names stay on your device. Companions keeps no account and never uploads your tank; see the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
