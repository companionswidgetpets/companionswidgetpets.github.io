Companions is free to download and free to keep using. The three stock fish and the five of the Freshwater Pack come with it, and so does everything else the app does: all eight tank styles, all the props, My Tanks, Explore, feeding, sharing and the widgets in every size.

The other fish come in **packs** of five, each a one-time purchase through the App Store. Buy a pack and it is yours for good, on every device signed in with your Apple Account.

**All Fish** opens every pack at once, including packs added in the future, and it comes two ways:

- **Lifetime**: one purchase, yours for good. It costs less than buying every pack separately.
- **Monthly**: a plan that runs while you want it. Cancel any time in your Apple Account's Subscriptions.

All Fish also unlocks the **complications**: the time, the date, the weather, your step goal or the Moon in a corner of a tank. If a monthly plan lapses, the fish you already added keep swimming and the complications you set keep working; only adding new fish from those packs, and choosing new complications, waits until it is renewed.

Prices depend on your country and are shown in the app: tap the **+** in the tray and then a locked pack, or open **Settings**. There are no other charges: no ads, no coins, no energy, nothing to keep the fish alive.
