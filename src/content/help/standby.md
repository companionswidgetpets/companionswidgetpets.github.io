Yes. StandBy is the full-screen view your iPhone shows when it is charging, locked and lying on its side, and its widget view takes the same small widgets as the Home Screen.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Make sure StandBy is on: <strong>Settings › StandBy</strong>.</li>
<li>Put your iPhone on a charger, on its side. StandBy appears.</li>
<li>Swipe sideways to the widget view (two widgets side by side).</li>
<li>Touch and hold one of the two widgets until it lifts, then tap <strong>+</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>Companions</strong>, then <strong>Add Widget</strong>, then <strong>Done</strong>.</li>
</ol>

The tank is the small tank, with its fish and props and the style you gave it, drawn full size beside the other widget. The **Dark** style was made with bedside tables in mind: it is as black as the screen allows, so the tank sits quietly in the dark.

On iPhones with an always-on display, StandBy stays visible overnight, dimming as the room darkens; other iPhones show it when you tap the screen or the table.
