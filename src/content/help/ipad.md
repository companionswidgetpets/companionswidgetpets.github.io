question: "Does Companions work on iPad?"
summary: "Yes. Companions is a native iPad app with the same Home Screen widgets in small, medium and large, and your packs come with you under the same Apple Account."

updated: 2026-11-09

Yes. Companions runs on iPad with the same app and the same three widget sizes, and iPadOS 26 or later. The tanks are laid out the same way as on iPhone, so a tank you design on one looks the same on the other.

Packs and All Fish are tied to your Apple Account, so anything bought on your iPhone is yours on your iPad: open **Settings** in the app and tap **Restore Purchases** if a pack shows as locked.

Tanks and fish themselves are kept on each device, not in the cloud. To bring a tank design across, use **Share this tank** on one device and open the code on the other; your fish are added on each device separately.
