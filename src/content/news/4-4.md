Companions 4.4 adds complications: a small, useful thing in a corner of the tank, borrowed from watch faces. Give any tank one, in any of its four corners.

- **Weather** shows the current conditions and the temperature.
- **Moon Phase** shows tonight's Moon, the right way up for your hemisphere.
- **Step Goal** shows your step goal and how far you've walked towards it, from Health.
- **Time** shows the time, to glance at.
- **Date** shows the day, the date and the month.
- **Bug fixes and performance improvements**, especially to the swim animations, from your feedback.

[Everything about complications](/blog/complications/) is in our guide.
