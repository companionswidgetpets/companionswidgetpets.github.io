Companions 4.0 brings the tank to your wrist.

- **An Apple Watch app.** Keep your tank, with all its fish, on your watch.
- **The same swim everywhere.** Fish move exactly as they do in the app and in the widget, and you can feed them on the watch too.
- **Watch Preview.** See exactly how your tank and everything in it will look on the watch, from the Watch Preview page in the app.
- **Bug fixes and performance improvements** from your feedback.

Our [Apple Watch guide](/blog/apple-watch/) shows how to set it up.
