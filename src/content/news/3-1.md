- **Save your tank designs** and switch between them whenever you like.
- **Share a design** with friends, as a QR code or a picture.
- **One tap to import.** A friend who opens your code gets your tank exactly as you made it, props and all, in their own collection of tanks.
- **Two new waters**: Blossom pink and Dark black.
- **Bug fixes and performance improvements** from your feedback.
