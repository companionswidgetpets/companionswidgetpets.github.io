Companions 4.5 is about having more than one tank that is truly yours.

- **A different set of fish for every tank size.** The small, medium and large tanks each keep their own five fish. Swipe to a tank and the tray shows its fish; add and remove them there. A small blue reef tank full of reef fish and a wide green pond tank full of freshwater fish, on the same Home Screen, is now possible.
- **Help for first-timers.** A few short notes point out the ⋯ menu and the + the first time you open the app, then stay out of the way.
- **Better starting tanks.** A fresh install starts with the first ready-made small tank, a bare Pond medium tank and a bare Lavender large tank, so each size is ready for its own fish.
- **Bug fixes and performance improvements** from your feedback. Thank you for writing.

Your existing fish stay where they were, in the small tank; the medium and large tanks start empty, ready for whatever suits them.
